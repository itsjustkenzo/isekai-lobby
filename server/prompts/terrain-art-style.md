# Detailed fantasy pixel-art environment

Create environment artwork belonging to the same game as the supplied detailed male knight and female mage references.

- Use the character references ONLY for pixel treatment, coloured outlines, coordinated colour ramps, light direction, material shading and deliberate detail. Never draw people, faces, eyes, limbs or their costumes into scenery.
- Use crisp square pixel clusters, stepped contours and selective dark coloured outlines. Avoid thick black cartoon borders, blurry edges, painted gradients, glossy 3D surfaces and smooth vector shapes.
- Materials must be distinct: leaves have individually shaded foliage clusters; bark has restrained grain; rock has facets and cracks; metal has sharp highlights; wood and fabric have controlled surface detail.
- Keep the pixel density consistent between ground, trees, plants, rocks and constructed props. Larger nature variants contain additional authored detail, not enlarged copies of smaller sprites.
- Match the described biome and season. Grasslands retain natural green, earth and foliage palettes; deserts use their own sand and plant colours. Do not force royal burgundy, navy, gold, armour motifs or silver hair colours onto every environment just because they appear on the character references.
- Preserve readable silhouettes and the supplied native sprite dimensions. These assets still target the existing game's tile and collision system. Do not change footprints or upscale old sprites to pretend they contain new detail.
- Ground and floor paint are flat top-down opaque materials, seamless at all four edges. Wall paint is a straight-on vertical material face. Never bake props, scenery, perspective, cast shadows, light beams or large repeating highlights into a material tile.
- Props use the game's slightly elevated front view, with bottom-centred bases, complete uncropped silhouettes and genuine transparency. No ground patches or baked cast shadows behind isolated props.
- Ignore the references' black backgrounds and glow. No halo, text, watermark, labels, sprite sheet or complete scene.

When a creator provides an interior picture, it determines subject matter, materials, palette and furniture design. Character references provide the shared pixel-art treatment; do not copy their subject matter into the interior assets.
